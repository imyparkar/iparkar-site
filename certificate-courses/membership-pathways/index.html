Membership pathways.

Turn your qualifications and experience into a professional membership grade — what each one requires and how to apply.

How the routes connect

  1. Level 3 certificateNEBOSH IGC or IOSH Level 3
  2. Level 6 diploma or degree+ 2 years’ experience
  3. CertIOSHCertified
  4. CMIOSHChartered · PDA
  5. CFIOSH5 years as CMIOSH

Occupational hygiene: BOHS modules or an accredited degree + 3 years’ experience → ICertOH and Licentiate of the Faculty of Occupational Hygiene.

IOSHCertIOSH

Certified membership

Health and safety professionals ready to lead in their organisation, managing strategy and operations.

Requirements

  • A Level 6 health and safety qualification — such as the IOSH Level 6 Diploma, the NEBOSH Diploma or an occupational safety and health degree — or a related degree (e.g. engineering or environmental science) with an OSH conversion qualification.
  • At least two years of professional experience.
  • Evidence of continuing professional development (CPD).
  • IOSH can assess qualifications from any country and advise whether more study is needed.

How to apply

  1. Join IOSH, or log in if you are already a member.
  2. Apply to upgrade to Certified membership with your qualification, experience and CPD evidence.
  3. Use CertIOSH after your name once approved.
Fees
Annual membership £183. Your employer may cover membership and assessment fees.
Next step
Build strategic experience, then take the Professional Development Assessment to reach Chartered. Most Certified members progress to CMIOSH within two to four years.

IOSHCMIOSH

Chartered membership

Experienced professionals with at least three years in senior roles; Chartered members may call themselves a Chartered Safety and Health Practitioner.

Requirements

  • Current IOSH membership — Affiliate, Technical or Certified.
  • Senior-level experience: strategic responsibility, advising leadership and leading organisation-wide initiatives.
  • Competence across technical knowledge, strategic thinking, leadership, ethics and CPD.
  • Usually a degree-level (UK Level 6 or equivalent) qualification; without one, extensive senior experience may be considered.

How to apply

  1. Confirm you meet the membership, experience and competence requirements.
  2. Choose a route: the Professional Development Assessment (PDA) for Certified members with approved qualifications (up to 18 months), or the Experiential route for those with five years’ senior experience and no degree-level OSH qualification (3–6 months).
  3. Complete the knowledge assessment, build your evidence portfolio and attend the professional discussion interview.
  4. If unsuccessful you receive detailed feedback and may reapply. Requirements are the same wherever you are based.
Fees
Annual membership £207. PDA route £550; Experiential route £1,080. Financial support is available for eligible members.
Next step
After five years as CMIOSH in senior leadership, you can apply for Chartered Fellowship.

IOSHCFIOSH

Chartered Fellow

IOSH’s highest grade, for established OSH leaders whose impact reaches beyond their own organisation. Open to international members.

Requirements

  • Chartered membership (CMIOSH) held for at least five years.
  • At least five years in senior health and safety leadership roles.
  • Strategic influence at executive or board level.
  • Outstanding contribution and influence — e.g. research, publications, speaking, mentoring or shaping standards and policy — with ethical practice and ongoing CPD.

How to apply

  1. Prepare a CV covering the last 10 years.
  2. Obtain two supporting statements from senior professionals.
  3. Write a personal strategy statement and gather evidence of CPD, ethical practice, impact and contributions.
  4. Submit the portfolio; it is assessed against seven criteria including leadership, integrity, influence and future contribution. IOSH may ask for more evidence or an interview.
  5. If unsuccessful, IOSH gives feedback, improvement guidance and a timescale to reapply.
Fees
Annual membership £227. Assessment fee £435, payable in full on registration.
Next step
Join the community of Fellows, with Fellows-only forums and events and a voice in IOSH strategy.

BOHSICertOH

International Certificate in Occupational Hygiene

Occupational hygiene practitioners who want to show knowledge and competence in the broad principles and practice of occupational hygiene. Level 6 in the BOHS framework.

Requirements

  • Successful completion of the appropriate BOHS occupational hygiene modules, or a BOHS-accredited degree.
  • Three years of relevant professional experience.
  • A computer and internet access — several components are completed and assessed online.

How to apply

  1. Create an account on the BOHS hub.
  2. Pay for each component through the hub.
  3. Compile your Personal Learning Portfolio (PLP) using the hub templates and submit it.
  4. Book and complete the professional discussion.
  5. View feedback and results in the hub.
Fees
Each component is paid separately through the BOHS hub; BOHS lists the current fees.
Next step
Use ICertOH after your name and join the Faculty of Occupational Hygiene as a Licentiate.
