NEBOSHSCQF Level 6 · comparable to RQF Level 3

NEBOSH International General Certificate in Occupational Health and Safety

A foundational qualification for managers, supervisors and people beginning an HSE career.

Entry
No formal entry requirements; suitable language skills are needed. NEBOSH recommends English equivalent to IELTS 6.0 for English study.
Study
Register and study with a NEBOSH accredited Learning Partner. Confirm the current syllabus, assessments and fees with your provider.
Next step
Build practical experience and consider advanced qualifications or a professional certification when you meet its prerequisites.

IOSHLevel 3

IOSH Level 3 Certificate

For new and aspiring OSH professionals, and supervisors and managers with safety responsibilities.

Entry
No formal entry requirements stated.
Units
1. OSH principles and management systems · 2. Risk management · 3. Incident management · 4. Improving OSH performance and culture.
Assessment
An 80-question multiple-choice exam (2 hours, 70% pass mark) and a scenario-based assessment (2 hours, 50% pass mark) covering an inspection, a risk assessment, an incident investigation and a toolbox talk. Both online with remote invigilation.
Leads to
Technical membership of IOSH (TechIOSH), and a strong base for the IOSH Diploma.
