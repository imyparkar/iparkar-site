Before you choose

Does one purchase include every certification?

Each purchase covers one selected exam: ASP, CSP, CIH, GES/EPI or QEP. The same three access durations are offered for each exam.

Will I be charged again automatically?

No. These are one-time purchases with 90, 180 or 365 days of access. There is no automatic renewal.

How do I get a 180-day or 365-day package?

Choose your certification and select Request access. Your email app will open a request to support@iparkar.com with the exam, duration and price included. We will help you arrange access; sending a request does not make a payment.

Where can I find the refund policy?

Read our refund policy before purchasing. For purchase questions, contact iParkar Learning.
