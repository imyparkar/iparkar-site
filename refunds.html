Refund policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

We want you to be happy with your purchase. If a practice exam isn’t right for you, you can get your money back.

1. 14-day money-back guarantee

You can request a full refund within 14 days of purchase, for any reason.

2. How to request a refund

  • Email support@iparkar.com with the email address you used to buy and your order or payment ID (shown in your confirmation email).

iParkar Learning is the seller of all our products and handles refunds directly. Payments are processed by our payment partner Razorpay, and approved refunds are issued through Razorpay to your original payment method. We will reply within 3 business days. Once your refund is approved and issued, we will email you a confirmation with a refund reference number. You can give this number to your bank if you need to trace the money.

3. What happens next

  • Refunds can only go back to the payment method you used to buy (the same card, UPI account, bank account or wallet). We cannot send a refund to a different card or account.
  • After we issue a refund, the time it takes to reach you depends on your payment method and your bank:
    • UPI: usually 2–7 business days
    • Net banking: usually 2–10 business days
    • Wallets: usually 0–3 business days
    • Credit and debit cards, including international cards: usually 5–10 business days. Some card issuers take longer to show the credit on your statement.
  • We refund the full amount you paid, or the agreed amount for a pro-rata refund. If you paid with a card in another currency, your bank converts the refund back, so the amount on your statement may differ slightly because of exchange rates. Any fees your own bank or card issuer charged are outside our control.
  • If you have not received your refund 10 business days after our confirmation email, contact your bank with the refund reference number, then email us and we will follow it up with Razorpay.
  • Your access to the refunded product ends when the refund is issued. Your past results stay in your account.

4. Failed or duplicate payments

  • If money left your account but your payment failed, or you did not get access, the payment is not completed and the amount is refunded automatically to your original payment method, usually within 5 business days plus your bank’s processing time. You do not need to request it.
  • If you were accidentally charged twice for the same order, the extra payment is refunded automatically in the same way.
  • If an automatic refund has not reached you within 10 business days, email support@iparkar.com with your payment ID or a bank statement showing the debit.

5. After 14 days

After 14 days we don’t normally offer refunds, because you have had full access to the material. We will still refund you if:

  • you were charged twice or charged in error;
  • a technical fault on our side stopped you using the Service and we could not fix it; or
  • we permanently withdraw the product during your access period (pro-rata refund).

Our payment partner cannot return money to the original payment method for payments more than 6 months old. If you are due a refund on such a payment (for example, a pro-rata refund on longer access), we will contact you and pay it by bank transfer instead.

6. When refunds may be refused

We may refuse a refund where there is clear evidence of abuse — for example, repeated buy-and-refund cycles, or an account closed for copying or sharing content under our Terms.

7. Your statutory rights

This policy does not affect any rights you have under the consumer laws of the country where you live.
