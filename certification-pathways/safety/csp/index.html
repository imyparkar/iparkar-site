CSP — Certified Safety Professional®

BCSP’s flagship credential for safety leaders who run safety management systems and shape culture.

BCSP · checked 24 Oct 2026

Official page

Requirements

  1. At least a bachelor’s degree.
  2. Four years of safety, health and environment experience, at least half of it preventive, professional-level work with breadth and depth of safety duties.
  3. A BCSP qualified credential — for example ASP, GSP, TSP, CIH, CMIOSH/CFIOSH, CRSP, NEBOSH National or International Diploma, or an AICTE-recognised Diploma/Certificate in Industrial Safety (India). See BCSP’s page for the full list.
  4. Pass the CSP examination.
  5. Keep the credential active: annual renewal fee plus recertification points every five years.
  6. Degrees from outside the United States are evaluated for U.S. equivalency; all education requirements must be met.

Application process

  1. Log in to BCSP’s “My Profile” portal and start a CSP application from the Applications menu.
  2. Upload evidence of your degree, experience and qualified credential, then pay the application fee.
  3. Once approved, you have one year to take and pass the exam.
  4. Buy the exam (US$350 for a single attempt at the time of checking — confirm current fees on BCSP’s At-A-Glance).
  5. Schedule with Pearson VUE through single sign-on and sit the computer-based exam; results are immediate.
  6. Retakes must be at least six weeks after your previous attempt.

Exam preparation

  1. Map your study plan to the CSP blueprint domains and their weightings.
  2. Expect scenario-based questions on safety management systems, risk assessment and leadership — not just recall.
  3. Refresh the maths: rates, statistics, ventilation, noise and engineering economics.
  4. Finish with full-length timed mocks to build exam stamina.
