Certification or certificate?

Choose by what you need to demonstrate, not just the name on the award.

Professional certification and taught qualifications compared
What to compareProfessional certificationCertificate or diploma course
PurposeValidates professional knowledge and competence against a credentialing body's standard.Develops and assesses learning against a course or qualification syllabus.
Typical entryMay require a degree, relevant work experience and an existing credential.Depends on the programme; some have no formal entry requirements.
AssessmentUsually a certification examination and review of eligibility evidence.May include exams, assignments, practical tasks or attendance requirements.
Keeping it currentOften involves renewal fees, continuing development and recertification.The award usually records achievement at a point in time; provider rules vary.
ExamplesASP, CSP, CIH, CFPS and QEP.NEBOSH International General Certificate, IOSH Level 3 Certificate and Level 6 diplomas.

A certificate of attendance is different from an assessed qualification. NEBOSH and IOSH qualifications assess learning; a course does not automatically award a professional certification. EPI is an interim designation towards QEP, not a certification. Always check the awarding body's terminology and recognition.
