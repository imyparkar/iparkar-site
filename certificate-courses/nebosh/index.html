NEBOSHSCQF Level 6 · comparable to RQF Level 3

International General Certificate in Occupational Health and Safety

A foundational qualification for managers, supervisors and people beginning an HSE career.

Entry
No formal entry requirements; suitable language skills are needed. NEBOSH recommends English equivalent to IELTS 6.0 for English study.
Study
Register and study with a NEBOSH accredited Learning Partner. Confirm the current syllabus, assessments and fees with your provider.
Next step
Build practical experience and consider advanced qualifications or a professional certification when you meet its prerequisites.

NEBOSH RQF Level 6 · 42 credits

NEBOSH Level 6 International Diploma for Occupational Health and Safety Management Professionals

For health and safety professionals, and those aiming to become one, who need in-depth knowledge.

Entry
None mandatory. The NEBOSH General Certificate is strongly recommended, and IELTS 7.0+ is recommended.
Units
DI1 Workplace health and safety principles (assignment, about 4 weeks) · DI2 Controlling workplace health issues (case study, about 2 weeks) · DI3 Controlling workplace safety issues (case study, about 2 weeks). Each is followed by a closing interview.
Study time
Minimum 185 taught hours, about 144 self-study hours and 80 assessment hours. Five years to complete all units.
Leads to
Meets the academic requirements for IOSH membership at the appropriate grade, and is a BCSP-recognised qualified credential towards the CSP.

NEBOSH SCQF Level 10 · comparable to RQF Level 6

NEBOSH International Diploma in Environmental Management

For aspiring environmental managers and safety practitioners who also manage environmental responsibilities.

Entry
No formal requirements. The NEBOSH Certificate in Environmental Management (or equivalent) is highly recommended, and IELTS 7.0+ is recommended.
Units
ED1 Controlling environmental aspects (3-hour written exam, 5 of 8 questions) · IDEM2 Environmental regulation (about 8,000-word assignment).
Study time
Minimum 119 taught hours and about 112 hours of private study. Complete both units within five years.
Leads to
Specialist membership of IIRSM (SIIRSM) and the letters IEnvDipNEBOSH. Some UK universities accept it for MSc entry.
