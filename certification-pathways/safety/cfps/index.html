CFPS — Certified Fire Protection Specialist®

NFPA’s credential for professionals who reduce fire loss through applied fire protection knowledge.

NFPA · checked 24 Oct 2026

Official page

Requirements

  1. Eligibility combines education and fire-protection work experience. The exact routes are set out in NFPA’s CFPS Candidate Handbook — read it before you apply.

Application process

  1. The application steps, fees and exam rules are set out in the CFPS Candidate Handbook.
  2. Applications are made online through NFPA’s certification portal.

Exam preparation

  1. Follow NFPA’s official CFPS learning path.
  2. The exam is built on the NFPA Fire Protection Handbook — know its structure well.
  3. Practise mixed questions across fire science, detection, suppression and life safety.
